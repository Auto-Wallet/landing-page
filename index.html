Product tour

A wallet that respects your time.

Every screen earns its real estate. No tracking pixels, no analytics, no “upgrade to pro”. Just a minimal, fast wallet that gets out of your way.

01 / Confirm

See exactly what you're signing — or skip the screen entirely.

Every non-whitelisted transaction is simulated up-front: balance deltas, gas estimate, function name, raw JSON if you want it. Trust the site? Flip the toggle, and the next time it's a no-op.

  • Token-change simulation with SUCCESS / REVERT badge
  • Origin verified via Chrome's sender.origin, never the page
  • One-toggle “Trust this site” auto-creates the whitelist rule
02 / Home

Balances, tokens, accounts — at a glance.

Switch accounts from the header dropdown. Add custom ERC-20s by address — symbol & decimals auto-fetch. Send, receive, or swap cross-chain without ever leaving the popup.

  • Unlimited accounts, one shared master password
  • Token icons via Trust Wallet CDN with wan- prefix handling
  • Built-in Swap powered by xflows · same-chain & cross-chain
03 / Swap · powered by xflows

Cross-chain swaps,
right inside the wallet.

Tap Swap on the home screen and you'll land in a full cross-chain aggregator powered by xflows.wanchain.org. Pick any token on any supported chain — search by name, paste a contract address, or star your favorites for one-tap access next time.

  • Source and destination tokens across 13+ chains
  • Built-in token search · paste-an-address support
  • Starred chains pin to the top of the picker
04 / Swap · xflows quote & broadcast

Quote, route, and bridge — in one screen.

Once you pick the pair, xflows finds the best route — same-chain DEX or cross-chain bridge — and shows the quote, slippage, min-received, price impact, and chosen route up front. Hit Swap and you'll see the source-chain tx hash stream in live.

  • Live quote with min received and price impact
  • Configurable slippage: 0.5% / 1% / 3% / custom
  • Source tx hash with explorer link, real-time progress

Security

Speed without
compromise.

Auto-signing only works if it's safer than what you're doing today. Every shortcut has an explicit safety net.

01

AES-256-GCM + PBKDF2 (600k)

Private keys are encrypted with your master password at rest. Key derivation uses 600,000 iterations — well above OWASP's 2024 recommendation.

02

Origin spoofing protection

Every dApp message tag's its source origin from Chrome's runtime, not the page. Strict equality match — no prefix tricks like uniswap.org.evil.com.

03

HTTPS-only chain adds

wallet_addEthereumChain requires explicit user approval, and the RPC URL must be HTTPS. No silent network mutation.

04

Signer address validation

When you have multiple accounts, the wallet refuses to sign on behalf of an account that isn't the active one — even if a dApp asks.

05

Gas & value caps

Whitelist rules always enforce per-rule gas-limit and value caps. A compromised dApp can't drain you through an auto-signed call.

06

No telemetry, ever

No analytics, no crash reports, no “help us improve” beacons. The extension makes RPC calls and nothing else. Inspect the source on GitHub.
