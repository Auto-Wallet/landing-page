Headline features

Three superpowers no other wallet ships.

Auto-signing is only safe when you can see what you're about to sign, only useful when you can also watch wallets you don't own, and only complete when you can move value across chains without bouncing through a separate dApp. Auto Wallet gives you all three.

#01 · Tripwire

See every balance change before you sign.

Every transaction — manual or auto — is replayed on a fork first. Auto Wallet returns the signed delta for every address and every ERC-20 involved. If an unknown address quietly gains value, or a token you didn't expect moves, the tripwire fires and routes the tx to manual approval instead. No more blind-signing a "swap" that secretly drains an approval.

#02 · View-only

Watch any wallet. Zero keys required.

Paste an address — that's it. Auto Wallet adds it as a watch-only account you can switch into from the header, with full token balances, history, and dApp connection support (read-only RPC calls answer normally; signing requests are blocked at the provider). Pair it with a hardware wallet for a hot/cold split, monitor a vault, or spy on your favorite whale — without ever holding their keys.

#03 · Cross-chain

Swap across chains without leaving the popup.

Auto Wallet ships a built-in cross-chain Swap tab powered by the xflows.wanchain.org aggregator API. Pick a token on any source chain, a token on any destination chain, and Auto Wallet handles the quote, the ERC-20 approval (with USDT-style allowance reset when needed), the source-chain tx, and tracks the bridge/swap to completion — all inside the popup. No tab-hopping, no copy-pasting bridge UIs, no signing on an unfamiliar site.
